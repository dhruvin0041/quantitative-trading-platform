PART 1: INDIA (IST) DAILY TRADING SCHEDULE
================================================================================
Because US markets operate in New York (Eastern Time), trading US stocks from 
India has a major natural advantage: you do not have to watch the market in the 
middle of the night. You have the entire morning and afternoon in India to review 
confirmed signals at your own pace with zero hurry.

US Daylight Saving Time changes the clocks by 1 hour twice a year:

+----------------------------------+-------------------------+-----------------------------------+------------------------------------------+
| Period                           | US Market Closes        | Best Time to Check in India (IST) | US Market Opens (Trade Fill in India)    |
|                                  | (Signal Confirmed)      |                                   |                                          |
+----------------------------------+-------------------------+-----------------------------------+------------------------------------------+
| Current (EDT: March to November) | 01:30 AM IST (Midnight) | Morning: 07:00 AM – 12:00 PM IST  | 07:00 PM IST (19:00 Evening)             |
| Winter  (EST: November to March) | 02:30 AM IST (Midnight) | Morning: 07:00 AM – 12:00 PM IST  | 08:00 PM IST (20:00 Evening)             |
+----------------------------------+-------------------------+-----------------------------------+------------------------------------------+

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE IDEAL INDIA DAILY ROUTINE (IST)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. MORNING (08:00 AM – 12:00 PM IST): REVIEW SIGNALS (Takes ~30 seconds)
   • Wake up, open browser to: http://localhost:3000
   • The US market closed hours ago. Data is 100% finalized and confirmed.
   • Look at the AAPL chart: Did a new BUY or SELL arrow appear on yesterday's bar?
   • If HOLD (no new arrow): You are done for the day! (No action needed).
   • If new BUY or SELL: Note the trade direction.

2. DAYTIME (FREE TIME & ORDER PLACEMENT)
   • You have all day until evening with zero rush.
   • Log into your broker app (e.g., Interactive Brokers, Vested, INDmoney, Schwab).
   • Enter a Market-on-Open (MOO) order or queue a standard Market order.
   • Sizing: 100% Cash / 100% Stock (Flat, Zero Margin, Zero Borrowing).

3. EVENING (07:00 PM IST Summer / 08:00 PM IST Winter): EXECUTION
   • The US market opening bell rings.
   • Your order executes automatically at the market open.
   • Open your broker app to confirm your execution fill price.

PART 2: MODEL TRAINING & OPTIMIZATION ROUTINE
================================================================================
In quantitative systematic trading, YOU SHOULD NEVER RETRAIN DAILY. Retraining 
every day causes high parameter churn, overfits to short-term noise, and harms 
live performance. Follow this institutional schedule:

+-------------------------------+----------------------+------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------+
| Task                          | Frequency            | Purpose                                  | When to Do It                                   |
+-------------------------------+----------------------+------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------+
| Signal Inference (Scan)       | Daily                | Generate live signals for today's trade  | Automatic via running FastAPI backend.          |
| Software Integrity Test       | Weekly (Saturdays)   | Verify zero bugs, parity & no lookahead  | Every weekend before Monday open.               |
| Model Retraining (Weights)    | Monthly or Quarterly | Incorporate latest 20–60 days of data    | First weekend of month or post-earnings.        |
| Hyperparameter Optimization   | Every 3 to 6 Months  | Re-tune tree depth, ATR bands & features | Biannually or during major macro regime shifts. |
+-------------------------------+----------------------+------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------+

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP-BY-STEP TRAINING & MAINTENANCE GUIDE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. WEEKLY HEALTH CHECK (Every Saturday, ~1 minute)
   Before each new trading week, run the automated test suite to confirm zero 
   software regression or lookahead leakage:

   powershell:
   cd d:\DataScience\Projects\Data_Science_Projects\Stock_Indicator\backend
   .\venv\Scripts\python.exe -m unittest tests/test_replay_live_parity.py
   .\venv\Scripts\python.exe -m pytest tests/test_live_signals_and_lookahead.py
   .\venv\Scripts\python.exe -m unittest tests/test_causality_and_execution_timing.py
   .\venv\Scripts\python.exe -m unittest tests/test_prospective_integrity.py

   *If all pass with OK, your system is mathematically verified for the week.*

2. MONTHLY RETRAINING ROUTINE (First Saturday of the Month, ~10 minutes)
   a. Clear old artifact cache to prevent weight contamination:
      powershell:
      python scripts/ops/clean_artifacts.py

   b. Re-run multi-modal training pipeline (XGBoost, LSTM, CNN ensemble).

   c. Verify all model checkpoints:
      powershell:
      python scripts/ops/verify_all_models.py

   d. Restart the FastAPI backend server to load updated model artifacts:
      powershell:
      python -m uvicorn api:app --host 0.0.0.0 --port 8000

3. BIANNUAL OPTIMIZATION ROUTINE (Every 6 Months)
   • Run Optuna hyperparameter sweeps across feature windows and Chandelier ratchets.
   • Always evaluate on Walk-Forward Analysis (WFA) out-of-sample data.
   • Target Metric: Maximize Jensen's Alpha and Sortino Ratio (not raw trade count).
   • Lock in stable parameter sets that survive multiple rolling market regimes.
